How it works

Find a visible label in three steps.

Use it when remote desktops, canvas interfaces, or older software do not provide labels to your screen reader.

  1. 01

    Choose one window

    Lens lists visible windows. You explicitly pick the one it may capture.

  2. 02

    Read visible labels

    Built-in optical character recognition (OCR) reads visible labels in reading order.

  3. 03

    Find a visible label

    Type “Save” and hear “bottom right.” Likely buttons are clearly marked as estimates.

How Lens handles captures

Capture pixels are not returned after recognition.

After recognition, Lens returns labels and directions, not capture pixels. You choose a window before capture.

Before you install

Product limits and recovery steps.

Can Lens click a button for me?

No. It gives a directional cue and keeps you in control. It does not move the pointer, press keys, or operate the target app.

Does OCR always get the label right?

No. Lens identifies every result as OCR text instead of showing an invented percentage. It may miss or misread small, stylized, moving, or low-contrast text. Enlarge the window or increase contrast and capture again.

Can I use the website sample offline?

Yes. Visit the sample once while online, then reload it without a connection.
