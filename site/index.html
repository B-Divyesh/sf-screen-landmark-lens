Before you install

Honest boundaries.

Can Lens click a button for me?

No. It gives a directional cue and keeps you in control. It does not move the pointer, press keys, or operate the target app.

Does OCR always get the label right?

No OCR does. Lens shows uncertainty and may miss small, stylized, moving, or low-contrast text. Enlarge the window or increase contrast and capture again.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Window capture and OCR are fully local. Internet is needed only to buy or verify an optional Plus license; a cached valid license continues optimistically offline.
