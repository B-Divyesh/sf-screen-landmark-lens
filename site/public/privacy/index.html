Plain-language policy · September 1, 2026

Your screen stays on your device.

Screen Landmark Lens processes the window you explicitly select on your own computer.

Window captures

A capture stays in memory only while the bundled OCR engine reads it. It is not written to your documents or sent to us.

We do not use it for training or retain it after analysis. Lens captures only the window you choose, even when that window is full-screen.

Local preferences

The app stores your voice speed locally. The sample project uses a separate demo storage key.

Choosing Start for real clears that demo preference and restores your regular app preference.

Website

The website has no advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, or third-party scripts. Its service worker caches public pages for offline use.

The landing page may ask GitHub’s API for release details once per hour in each browser. GitHub receives ordinary request data for that check. The sample demo never makes that request.

Your choices

You can use all shipped label-finding features without an account or purchase. You can revoke screen-capture permission in your operating-system settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public project repository. Do not include sensitive captures.
