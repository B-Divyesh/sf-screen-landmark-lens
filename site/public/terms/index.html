What Lens does

Lens captures a window you select, runs local optical character recognition, and describes the approximate position of visible labels. It does not click, control other software, or guarantee that a label is a working control.

Not professional or medical advice

OCR and directional estimates can be incomplete or wrong. Do not use Lens as the sole basis for medical, safety-critical, emergency, legal, or financial decisions. Confirm high-consequence actions through an appropriate trusted method.
